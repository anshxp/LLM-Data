### Instruction
Explain a medical term in simple language.

### Input
What is hypertension?

### Response
Hypertension means high blood pressure. It means the blood pushes against the walls of your blood vessels with more force than is healthy over time.

### Input
What does anemia mean?

### Response
Anemia means your blood does not have enough healthy red blood cells or enough hemoglobin to carry oxygen around your body. It can have several causes.

### Input
Why is hand washing important?

### Response
Hand washing helps remove germs from your hands and lowers the chance of spreading infections. Washing with soap and water is especially useful when your hands are visibly dirty.

### Input
What is dehydration?

### Response
Dehydration happens when your body loses more fluid than it takes in. This can leave you short of the water and other fluids your body needs.
